### **GRAV-COLLAPSE**
*Spacetime Orbital Matcher*

---

### **Summary**
Welcome to **GRAV-COLLAPSE**, an immersive cosmic puzzle-action game where you manipulate gravity to manage celestial elements on a warping spacetime grid. 

Drifting elements (from Hydrogen to Iron Cores) are drawn toward the central Singularity. Your goal is to merge matching elements smoothly, build massive combos, feed high-tier Iron Cores into the core, and prevent space dust buildup. Let the instability gauge hit 100%, and the system will trigger a catastrophic Supernova!

---

### **How to Play**
- **Merge Elements:** Combine two identical elements at soft speeds to fuse them into higher-tier elements (Hydrogen ➔ Helium ➔ Carbon ➔ Oxygen ➔ Iron Core).
- **Avoid Fractures:** High-speed impacts or collisions between mismatched elements will shatter them into Space Dust, increasing your system's instability.
- **Feed the Singularity:** Guide final-tier **Iron Cores (Fe)** into the central Singularity to score massive points and vent system instability.
- **Manage Instability:** Keep an eye on your stability bar. Too much Space Dust will trigger warnings and eventually a game-ending Supernova.

---

### **Controls**
- **Hold Left-Click:** Attract surrounding elements and spacetime toward your cursor's gravity well.
- **Hold Right-Click (or Shift):** Repel elements and push them away from your cursor.
- **Spacebar:** Detonate the *Singularity Pulse* to instantly clear out troublesome Space Dust (Subject to cooldown).
