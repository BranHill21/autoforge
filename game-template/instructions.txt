Neon Necromancer

A fast-paced, reverse bullet hell where you control a squishy Necromancer who cannot attack directly. You are dropped into an arena filled with deadly environmental hazards and swarming enemies. 

You must dodge and kite enemies into the traps to kill them. Every enemy that dies instantly resurrects as a loyal, glowing undead minion that orbits you, forming a protective, offensive meat-shield.

Features:
- Survive waves of endless enemies by turning their fallen comrades against them.
- Gather Soul Shards to purchase persistent meta-upgrades and increase your starting power.
- High-intensity synthwave visuals and geometry-based scaling swarm mechanics.

How to Play:
1. Use W, A, S, D (or Arrow Keys) to move your Necromancer.
2. Kite red enemy squares into the orange spinning traps to kill them.
3. Collect the yellow XP diamonds that enemies drop to level up during a run.
4. Grow your skeleton swarm to destroy enemies directly.

Controls:
- W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys - Move
- Mouse click - UI Interaction
