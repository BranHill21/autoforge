# TEMPORAL COURIER

**Draw splines. Deliver packages. Leave Echoes behind.**

---

### BRIEF SUMMARY
Step into a retro-futuristic wireframe dimension where time is your greatest asset and your most dangerous obstacle. As a Temporal Courier, your mission is to navigate high-risk sectors and deliver packages to the green portal. You have a 15-second time loop to analyze lethal hazards and plot a safe course. 

When your courier meets an untimely demise—or when you bake your run early—your past actions crystallize into solid **Echoes**. Use these temporal clones as stepping stones and weight-triggers to bypass security doors, survive escalating sectors, and secure your delivery!

---

### HOW TO PLAY
1. **Analyze:** Study the sector hazards (lasers and moving barriers) and locate the green **GATE** portal.
2. **Draw:** Click and drag your Mouse during the 15-second setup phase to draw a custom spline path for your courier to follow.
3. **Execute:** Watch your courier trace the path. Avoid running into hazards!
4. **Iterate:** Every time you crash or finish a run, your path is "baked" into a physical Echo. Use past Echoes to block hazards, trigger switches, and reach previously inaccessible areas.
5. **Progress:** Reach the green Portal before time collapses to advance to the next, increasingly challenging Sector!

---

### CONTROLS

* **Hold Left Mouse Button & Drag:** Draw your custom movement path.
* **Spacebar:** Bake current path / Force execution phase early & reset.
* **R Key:** Instant Reset / Restart current sector.
