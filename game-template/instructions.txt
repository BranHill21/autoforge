# TEMPORAL CUSTOMS INSPECTOR
**SYSTEM-STATUS: ONLINE // ERA: 2084**

---

### **Game Summary**
Welcome to the front lines of timeline security, Inspector! In **Temporal Customs Inspector**, you are tasked with managing cargo across three different eras: the Past, the Present, and the Future. Sort incoming artifacts, manage your timeline stability before time runs out, and avoid letting corrupted paradoxes collapse the entire space-time continuum. Do you have what it takes to climb the Temporal Archives and secure the multiverse?

---

### **How to Play**
- **Sort the Cargo:** Match items to their correct era core based on their color coding:
  - 🟢 **Green (Past Core):** Fossils, urns, and runestones.
  - 🟠 **Amber (Present Core):** Floppy disks, VHS tapes, and CRT monitors.
  - 🔵 **Cyan (Future Core):** Plasma cores, laser rifles, and dark matrices.
- **Beat the Clock:** Each shift gives you 30 seconds of stability. Pack all items and hit **Verify** before time runs out!
- **Beware Decay & Entanglement:** As you progress, unplaced items will begin to decay and glitch into random slots. Watch out for quantum-entangled items where rotating one rotates its linked partner!
- **Boss Anomalies:** Every 10 shifts, face the ultimate challenge—the Chronos Engine—which must be placed across all three timelines simultaneously.

---

### **Controls**
- **Left-Click:** Pick up / place items from the conveyor belt or grid cells.
- **R Key:** Rotate the currently held item (and any quantum-linked partners).
- **Spacebar:** Verify your completed shift and advance to the next wave.
- **Mouse Hover:** Inspect item dimensions, decay meters, and target placement zones.
