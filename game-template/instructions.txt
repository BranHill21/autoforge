# SUBTERRANEAN CLINIC

## Summary
Welcome to **Subterranean Clinic**, a high-stakes, cyberpunk medical management game where ethics are a luxury and survival depends on steady hands. Operating deep in the underground, your job is to manage illicit organ grafts, cater to desperate clientele, and keep your patients breathing while managing your bottom line. Move fast, satisfy your buyers, but watch out—every illegal procedure raises your **Police Heat**, and one wrong move means the authorities will shut your operation down permanently!

---

## How to Play
1. **Fulfill Client Demands:** Review the required organs for each patient on your operating table.
2. **Harvest from the Vat:** Drag the correct biomatter and organs from the **Biomatter Vat** on the left and drop them onto their matching surgery slots on the right. 
3. **Beat the Clock & Patient Stability:** Complete the grafts before the patient's stability hits 0% or the surgery timer runs out.
4. **Manage Police Heat:** Successful surgeries increase your credits, reputation, and **Police Heat**. If your heat gets too high between shifts, it's game over! Use bribes if necessary to keep the authorities off your back.
5. **Survive the Shifts:** Complete all clients in a shift to progress to harder, more lucrative contracts.

---

## Controls
* **Mouse / Touch:** Click and drag organs from the Biomatter Vat to the corresponding surgery slots on the Operating Table. Release to drop.
* **Click / Tap:** Interact with menu buttons, start shifts, and trigger emergency bribes.
