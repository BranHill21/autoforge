# GLYPHSTACK

### Stack glowing glyphs on the swaying pedestal!

**GLYPHSTACK** is a sleek, physics-based stacking challenge set in a glowing neon void. Test your precision and timing as you drop an array of uniquely shaped and colored glyphs onto a constantly swaying pedestal. As your tower grows taller and you climb through higher tiers, the pedestal sways wider, unpredictable wind gusts push at your blocks, and a single mistake can send your masterpiece tumbling into the abyss! 

Can you maintain your balance and build the ultimate cosmic tower, or will your stack collapse under pressure?

---

### How to Play
* **Position Your Drop:** Move your mouse left and right to align the crane over the pedestal.
* **Rotate for Balance:** Use the rotation controls to angle your glyph so it fits snugly against the pieces below it.
* **Stack Up:** Drop your glyphs carefully. Reaching higher elevation tiers increases your score multiplier, but beware—higher tiers introduce aggressive wind and heavier sway!
* **Save the Stack:** In tight spots, you have a limited supply of **Micro-Nudge Shockwaves** to blast falling or slipping pieces back into the air. 
* **Don't Let Them Fall:** If a dropped glyph falls off the screen or misses the platform, it's game over!

---

### Controls

* **Mouse X** — Position Crane
* **A / D or Left / Right Arrow Keys** — Rotate Glyph Counter-Clockwise / Clockwise
* **Mouse Scroll Wheel** — Fine-tune Glyph Rotation
* **Left Click / Spacebar** — Drop Glyph
* **Right Click** — Trigger Micro-Nudge Shockwave (Limited uses per run)
