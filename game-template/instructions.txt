**SINKHOLE SYNDICATE**
*Corporate Retro-Dystopian Destruction*

---

### **About the Game**
Welcome aboard, Employee #404. Management has reviewed your performance metrics, and you’ve been reassigned to our premier asset-liquidation division. Your task is simple: swallow office furniture, dodge rogue corporate drones, and meet your mandatory destruction quota before the shift clock runs out. Failure is not an option—unless you want to face immediate termination! 

Sinkhole Syndicate is a fast-paced, arcade-style physics game wrapped in a slick retro-dystopian terminal aesthetic. Expand your void, swallow the office, and cash in your stolen loot on the black market to upgrade your destructive capabilities.

---

### **How to Play**
1. **Clock In:** Start your shift and review your mandatory property damage quota ($).
2. **Swallow the Office:** Guide your sinkhole over desks, chairs, computers, and filing cabinets to drag them into the void and rack up your score.
3. **Manage Stability:** Expanding your hole drains your stability meter. Keep an eye on it, or you'll lose your pulling power!
4. **Watch out for Drones:** Avoid hitting corporate security drones, as collisions will damage your stability and ruin your workflow.
5. **Hit the Quota:** Reach your financial goal before the 60-second timer hits zero to advance to the next shift and cash in your loot at the Black Market.

---

### **Controls**
* **Move Mouse:** Aim / Move the sinkhole
* **Hold Left Click:** Expand the void (drains stability)
* **Right Click:** Execute a Sonic Burp (pushes away nearby props and drones)
* **Spacebar:** Pause / Unpause the game
